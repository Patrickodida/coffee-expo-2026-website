Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16–18 September 2026 | Speke Resort Munyonyo

About the Expo

The Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF) invites growers, exporters, roasters and investors to the Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026. Over three days at Speke Resort Munyonyo, the Expo will showcase Uganda's coffee from the farm to the cup.

Visitors can attend expert sessions, taste specialty coffees and meet buyers from around the world. Whether you are a smallholder farmer or an international trader, the Expo is the place to build partnerships and grow your business.
